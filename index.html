About Aurelia

Less noise. More thoughtful choices.

Aurelia is a front-end prototype for a modern lifestyle and fashion store. The experience is intentionally simple: clear navigation, useful product information, fast interactions, and a visual system that lets the products stay central.

The project is structured so authentication, product inventory, checkout, orders, and customer data can later be connected to Firebase, Supabase, or another backend without redesigning the storefront.
